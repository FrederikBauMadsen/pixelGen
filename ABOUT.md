# About me, and why this repo exists

I am Frederik. This is a note about my connection to programming, written down
so it stays with the code.

## What I love

I love writing code that is as optimised as possible for exactly what it needs
to do. Not more, not less. When code does its one job with nothing wasted, it
feels right to me.

I love it when code involves math or cryptic patterns that make the computation
shorter, cleaner and faster. A formula that replaces fifty lines. A pattern in
the numbers that lets the machine skip work. That is the part of programming
that intrigues me most.

I love writing math that runs fast and produces something you can see: images,
art, animations, effects. Rendering that is both quick and beautiful is the
thing I want to keep doing.

## What I hate

I hate code written to solve a future, unknown use case. Abstractions for
problems nobody has yet. Flexibility that costs speed and clarity today for a
tomorrow that may never come. I would rather write exactly what is needed now
and rewrite it when the need actually changes.

## Pixelgen

Pixelgen was my bachelor project. I wrote it directly from my dreams and my
mind. I have never been more intrigued in my life than when I wrote it.

The idea that pulled me in was being able to express my mind with math that
generated art: a crab built from layered pixel items, a superformula shaping
fire and stars along the border of a hat or a hand, particles composited onto
raw image data frame by frame. It rendered fast, and it looked like something
I had imagined.

I have a strong connection to this project because of where it came from.

## What comes next

I want to build more of this. Pixelgen is the seed. It evolves from here into
a new, private repo that is my own, where the focus is math that runs fast and
makes things you can see.

I want to start today, building my future.
